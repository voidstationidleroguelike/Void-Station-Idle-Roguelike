About the Game

Void Station is a fast-paced idle roguelike where you control a high-tech space station. Fight off endless waves of cosmic horrors, upgrade your weapons, and survive the deep dark of the void. Play automatically or take control to maximize your efficiency!

Global Competition

Compete with players worldwide! Our integrated Global Leaderboards let you see your rank across 9 different difficulty Tiers.

Cloud Save

Never lose your progress. Your station upgrades, currency, and achievements are synced across all your Android devices.

Data Transparency: Void Station uses Google Play Games Services solely to manage global leaderboards and sync game progress via Cloud Save, ensuring your player data is securely preserved. For a detailed breakdown of how data is handled, please review our full Privacy Policy.
