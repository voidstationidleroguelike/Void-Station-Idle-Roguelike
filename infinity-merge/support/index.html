Support

Need help with Infinity Merge? Here are the most common answers and how to contact us.

Ads are not loading

Check your internet connection and try again later. Rewarded bonuses are only granted after the rewarded ad has been successfully completed and confirmed by the ad provider.

Game progress

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Removing the app may remove locally stored progress.

Restart the game

Open the settings menu inside Infinity Merge and choose Restart Game.

Best in World

The global best score uses an online leaderboard service. If it does not update immediately, check your connection and try again later.
