Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

Infinity Merge does not require an account or login. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address or other account information to play the game.

Local game data

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. This information is used to keep your progress between sessions.

Advertising

Infinity Merge uses Google AdMob to provide banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information needed to deliver, measure and secure ads in accordance with your consent choices and Google's privacy practices.

Where required, the app uses Google's consent tools before requesting advertising.

Rewarded ads are optional. In-game rewards are only granted when the advertising provider confirms that the rewarded ad was successfully completed.

Online leaderboard

Infinity Merge uses Firebase for the global Best in World leaderboard. Limited gameplay and technical information may be transmitted as necessary to provide this feature.

Children

Infinity Merge is not specifically directed at children. The app does not require users to create an account or submit personal profile information.
