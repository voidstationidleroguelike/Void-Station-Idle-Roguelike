Infinity Merge

Swipe. Merge. Climb.

Simple to learn. Hard to master.

Infinity Merge is an endless number-merging puzzle. Swipe the board, combine matching tiles and build higher and higher values while managing your Moves and Gold.

1. Swipe Swipe in any direction to move the tiles across the board.
2. Merge Matching tiles combine into a higher-value tile.
3. Manage Moves Every valid swipe costs 1 Move. Each merge gives you +1 Move.
4. Upgrade Earn Gold, improve your spawn level and use Hammers when the board gets difficult.
