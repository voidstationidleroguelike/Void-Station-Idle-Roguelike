Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Overview

RDK Motion respects your privacy.

RDK Motion is designed to provide adaptive home workouts without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information directly to RDK Motion.

Workout Data

RDK Motion uses information about your workouts and preferences to provide features such as adaptive exercise targets, workout history and progress tracking.

This may include information such as:

  • Completed workouts
  • Exercise repetitions and durations
  • Workout preferences
  • Exercise difficulty feedback
  • Favorite or disabled exercises

This information is used to provide and personalize functionality inside the app.

Advertising

RDK Motion is free to use and is supported by banner advertising.

The app may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect certain information from your device in accordance with their own privacy policies.

This information may include device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, and information related to advertising interactions.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at:

Google Privacy Policy

No Subscription Required

RDK Motion does not require a subscription to use its workout features.

There are no hidden subscription fees.

Third-Party Services

Third-party services used by RDK Motion may process information according to their own privacy policies.

RDK Motion does not control how third-party services process information collected directly by those services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RDK Motion changes or when new services or features are introduced.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
