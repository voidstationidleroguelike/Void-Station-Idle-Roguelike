Privacy Policy for Void Station

Last Updated: May 2024

This game is designed with a privacy-first approach. We respect your data and aim to be transparent about the minimal information we use to provide a high-quality gaming experience.

1. Google Play Games Services & Cloud Save

To provide competitive and persistent gameplay, we utilize Google Play Games Services.

  • Leaderboards: When you are signed in, your highest sector reached (score) is sent to Google Play Services to be displayed on global leaderboards.
  • Cloud Save: We use the drive.appdata scope to securely store your game progress (upgrades, currency, and settings) in a private folder on your Google Drive. This data is only accessible by this game and is used to sync your progress across different devices.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

  • Firebase Analytics: We use Firebase to collect anonymous, aggregated data (such as device model, wave completion, and crashes). This is strictly used for game balancing and technical improvements.
  • Advertising ID: We may access your device's anonymous Advertising ID for attribution and analytics purposes to understand where our players come from.

3. In-App Purchases (IAP)

All financial transactions are handled exclusively by the Google Play Store. The developer never sees or stores your payment information.
