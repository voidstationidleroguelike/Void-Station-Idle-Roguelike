Privacy Policy for Void Station

Last Updated: May 2024

This game is designed with a privacy-first approach. We respect your data and aim to be transparent about the minimal information we use to provide a high-quality gaming experience.

1. Google Play Games Services & Cloud Save

To provide competitive and persistent gameplay, we utilize Google Play Games Services.

  • Leaderboards: When you are signed in, your highest sector reached (score) is sent to Google Play Services to be displayed on global leaderboards.
  • Cloud Save: We use the drive.appdata scope to securely store your game progress (upgrades, currency, and settings) in a private folder on your Google Drive. This data is only accessible by this game and is used to sync your progress across different devices. We do not have access to any other files on your Google Drive.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

  • Firebase Analytics: We use Firebase to collect anonymous, aggregated data (such as device model, wave completion, and crashes). This is strictly used for game balancing and technical improvements.
  • Advertising ID: We may access your device's anonymous Advertising ID for attribution and analytics purposes to understand where our players come from.

3. In-App Purchases (IAP)

All financial transactions are handled exclusively by the Google Play Store. The developer never sees or stores your payment information or credit card details.

4. Data Security & Retention

Your game data is stored locally on your device and/or encrypted on Google’s secure servers via Cloud Save. We do not maintain any independent database of your personal information.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our game is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Your Rights

You can at any time sign out of Google Play Games in the settings to stop data syncing or delete your Cloud Save data through your Google Account settings.
