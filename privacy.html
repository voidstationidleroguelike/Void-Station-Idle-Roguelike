Last Updated: June 2024

We respect your privacy. This game uses Google Play Games Services to provide global leaderboards and secure Cloud Save functionality.

Data Usage Transparency

  • Leaderboards: Your highest sector reached is shared with Google Play Services to calculate your global rank.
  • Progress Sync (Cloud Save): We use the drive.appdata scope to store your game settings in a private, isolated folder on your Google Drive. We cannot see or touch any other files in your drive.
  • Referral System: If you use an invite code, a unique anonymous ID for your device is stored in our database (Firebase Firestore) to track referral progress. This is only used to reward players when a friend reaches Sector 16. No names, emails, or personal identities are collected or shared.
  • Analytics: We use Firebase to track game crashes and balancing data (like which weapons are most popular) to improve the game.

Your Control

You can opt-out of data collection at any time by signing out of Google Play Games in the in-game settings menu. You can also delete your save data via your Google Account dashboard.

If you have any questions, feel free to contact us at voidstationidle@gmail.com.
