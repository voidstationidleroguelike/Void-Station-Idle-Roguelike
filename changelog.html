v3.6.3 - The Commander Teaser
July 2026

New Planned Content Teaser

  • Commanders System: Development has started on Commanders with levels, evolutions, and equipment items.
  • Loot Drops: Enemies will soon have a chance to drop unique items and equipment during gameplay.
  • Optional Support: Everything remains possible for F2P players, with optional IAP for those who wish to support the solo developer.

Global Rebalance

  • Boss Nerfs: Sector 5 and 10 Bosses have had their fire range and damage significantly reduced for a smoother start.
  • Combat Survival: Reduced collision damage for Tank, Exploder, and Shooter enemies to prevent instant-death spikes in early sectors.
  • Additive Scaling: Refined Tier 1 HP scaling to ensure game stability during extreme late-game progression.
  • Homing Weapons: Increased fuel/life for Missiles and Torpedoes, allowing them to hit targets within their full Range even in curved paths.

Fixes & UI

  • Auto-Pilot "Rusher": Fixed selection logic to correctly prioritize weapons under Level 10 for faster evolutions.
  • Level Up UI: HP and Shield bubbles now perfectly match the text percentages. Added green bubbles for weapon-specific Range upgrades.
  • Store Visibility: Interstellar Scout is now visible starting at Sector 15 instead of 100.
  • Translations: Updated descriptions for "Nano Repair" and "Shield Recharge" across all 8 languages to clarify they boost speed, not percentages of Max HP.
v3.6.0 - The Endless Expansion
June 2026

New Endless Roadmap & Rewards

  • Dynamic Reward Tiers: Earn massive credits based on your Best Run value:
    • 1 min: 20,000 Credits | 2 min: 100,000 Credits
    • 3 min: 0.5x Best Run | 4 min: 1x Best Run
    • 12 min: 2x Best Run | 22 min: 3x Best Run
  • Satellite Core Milestones: Reach 10m, 20m, and 30m to earn a free Satellite Core!

New Store & Community

  • First Advisor Pack: Exclusive starter pack added! Grants +100 HP, +100 Shield, 0.5x Multipliers and 3,000 Credits instantly.
  • Discord Promo Codes: Special global codes will now be shared exclusively in our Discord community. Join us to stay updated!

Auto-Pilot Overhaul

  • Weapon Priority: New "Weapon priority to level 10" toggle. Force the AI to max out your arsenal before taking any passive skills.
  • Improved Strategy: Station now continues to rotate smoothly during Level Up animations for a seamless visual experience.

Stability & Performance

  • Adreno GPU Fix: Total overhaul of the rendering engine. Capped all shadow effects to prevent stuttering and crashes on Android devices.
  • Stat Overflow Fix: Increased internal math limit to 1e200 to support extreme endgame scaling.
