v3.5.1 - Stability & "No-Crash" Safeguard
June 2026

Fixes & Safeguards

  • GPU Fix (Adreno): Removed heavy glow effects in Low Quality mode that previously overloaded graphics drivers on certain Android devices.
  • Crash Safeguard: The game now actively monitors your FPS. If it drops too low, Low Quality mode is forced automatically to save your run.
  • Projectile Cap: Introduced a hard limit of 600 simultaneous projectiles on-screen to ensure stable performance during extreme endgame battles.
v3.5.0 - The Evolution Update
June 2026

This is the biggest update in the game's history! We are introducing entirely new ways to play with the Auto-Pilot module, expanding the universe with Tiers 10-12, and completing a total technical overhaul to ensure the game runs silky smooth on all devices.

New Auto-Pilot Module

  • Automatic Level-up: The station will now choose upgrades for you during combat. Unlock it in the shop!
  • Full Control: Configure your own prioritization list inside the Codex (e.g., focus on DMG before HP).
  • Premium Pass Benefit: Premium Pass owners grant immediate access to advanced configuration options.

New Rifts: Tier 10, 11 & 12

  • Three brand-new challenges have opened up for the absolute strongest stations, requiring extreme strategy:
    • Tier 10: New Genesis (Starts with up to 150,000 HP)
    • Tier 11: Dimension Zero
    • Tier 12: Void Emperor

New Social & Rewards

  • Refer a Friend System: Introduced an invite-based reward system. Share your unique Invite Code (Codex -> Stats) to earn massive bonuses.
  • Recruitment Rewards: New players receive 3,000¢ instantly. Referrers receive a 3X Credit Chest when their friend reaches Sector 16.
  • Community Promo Codes: Added support for global codes. Use VOID2026 for a welcome gift!

Gameplay & Balancing

  • Altered Scaling: Enemies now become 3x stronger after Sector 500. Tier 8 and Tier 9 have received doubled growth rates to provide a true endgame challenge.
  • Anti-Immortality: Adjusted damage ratios for T6-12 (locked to 50% of base HP) ensuring enemies always remain a threat.
  • Rebirth 2.0: You now retain 0.1% of your Credits upon Rebirth for a faster headstart in your next life.
  • Pacifist Check: Runs will now automatically terminate if you reach Sector 4 without killing any enemies (Level 1 exploit fix).

Endless Void Improvements

  • MM:SS Records: Endless mode now saves your personal records by Time instead of sectors within the Codex.
  • Reward Roadmap: View exactly when rewards drop (1m, 2m, 4m, 10m, 20m) directly inside the difficulty selection menu.

User Interface (UI)

  • Unified 2x Speed: Combined the speed toggle into a single, clean layout with clearer information regarding the XP penalty.
  • Pause Status: The pause menu now clearly indicates if you are playing at double speed (e.g., "NORMAL 2x").
  • Centralized Localization: Migrated all translations to a brand-new system for faster updates and significantly less lag in menus.
  • S25 Support: UI layout adjusted for perfect scaling on modern narrow-screen devices.

Stability & Performance

  • ANR Protection: Triple-layer optimization to prevent "Application Not Responding" errors during high-intensity waves.
  • Multithreaded Cloud Saving: All Play Games operations now run on background threads with a 5s safety timeout to ensure smooth UI performance.
  • Engine Optimization: Migrated to squared-distance collision logic, reducing CPU load significantly.

Game Logic

  • Infinite Progression: Internal number limit increased to 1e100.
  • Advanced Metrics: Added "Credits Per Minute" and "Session Time" tracking to game-over summary.

📘 Player's Guide to 3.5

What is 2x Sector Speed? This is a mode that allows sectors to progress twice as fast. It is the quickest way to farm Credits, but keep in mind: because you defeat fewer enemies per sector, your level will be lower when facing bosses. Use this for farming, but toggle it off when aiming for a new high-sector personal record!
How do the new Tiers work? Tiers 10-12 are specifically designed for endgame players who have maxed out many of their satellite upgrades. Enemy strength escalates extremely fast here, requiring a precise balance of damage and defense to survive the first 100 sectors.
Why does my game look different now? If your game previously crashed or suffered from lag, the new Crash Safeguard system might have automatically lowered your graphics settings to Low. You can manually change it back to High in the settings menu if you are using a high-end device, but Low is now 100% stable across all devices.
